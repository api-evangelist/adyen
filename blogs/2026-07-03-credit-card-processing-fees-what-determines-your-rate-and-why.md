Credit card processing fees typically range from 1% to 3.5% of transaction value and consist of interchange, scheme fees, and processor markup, the only negotiable layer. The article notes the lowest quoted rate doesn't guarantee the lowest total cost, and suggests optimizing transaction routing, supporting multiple payment methods, and processing locally to reduce expenses.
