Based on a 2026 ProviderPay Study of 199 healthcare providers, this Adyen playbook shows how transitioning from standalone payment tools to an embedded model can cut weekly payment administration from 13.5 hours to 7.8 hours. The guide offers actionable strategies for healthcare software platforms to evolve their payment capabilities while improving provider retention and revenue generation.
